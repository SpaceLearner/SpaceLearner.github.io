WorldVLA

Co-author

Jointly models robot actions and future visual states autoregressively, using action attention masking to reduce error accumulation.

03 / ABOUT

About

Jiayan Guo

I work on post-training for general-purpose agents at Tencent. I focus on how agents organize tasks, use tools, and carry out multi-step workflows.

Previously, I worked on embodied intelligence at Alibaba DAMO Academy. I led the development of ZeroSearch, a training framework for search agents. I was also one of the project leads for RynnBrain, where I was responsible for model pre-training and post-training. My research covers search agents, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence.

WSDM 2023 Best Paper Honorable Mention; National Scholarship for Doctoral Students; Beijing Outstanding Graduate. View papers ↘
