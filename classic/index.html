Agents that plan, act, and learn

Jiayan Guo

I study agents that act: they plan, call tools, observe what happens, and adjust, over long multi-step tasks in digital and physical environments.

At Tencent, I work on general-purpose agents. Previously at Alibaba DAMO Academy on multimodal agents and embodied foundation models. Ph.D., Peking University.

01

Research

The question behind my work: what does an agent need to finish a long task on its own? It has to decide what to do next, act through tools or a body, and learn from the feedback.

  1. IAgentic search and tool use

    Search is an agent's most basic tool: decide what to look for, query, read, and decide again. ZeroSearch trains that loop with reinforcement learning against a simulated retriever, so the policy learns from interaction without a live search engine. InterLV-Search evaluates the same loop with images in it: can an agent use visual evidence to choose its next search step?

  2. IIEmbodied agents: from perception to action

    An embodied agent closes the same loop through a body: perceive, plan, act, and see the consequences. RynnBrain provides perception and planning: egocentric grounding in space and time, physical reasoning, and task plans. WorldVLA and the RynnVLA series turn plans into robot actions and predict what those actions will look like.

Across both, I focus on how agents learn from interaction feedback rather than static demonstrations, and how they organize tools and multi-step workflows. The long-term goal is agents that carry out long-horizon tasks reliably in complex environments.

Earlier work: generative decision-making, graph learning, and recommender systems.

02

Selected work

IAgentic search and tool use

ZeroSearch

Corresponding author · 2025

Trains LLM agents to search: the agent issues queries and reasons over the results across turns. During RL training a simulated retriever stands in for the search engine, supplying documents of controlled quality on a curriculum.

Paper ↗Code ↗

InterLV-Search

Co-author · NeurIPS 2026, Evaluations and Datasets Track

A benchmark for whether agents can use visual evidence to decide what to search next, not just to answer at the end. It spans three levels: active visual evidence seeking, controlled offline search, and open-web interleaved search.

Paper ↗Code ↗

IIEmbodied agents: from perception to action

RynnBrain

One of the project leads · 2026

An open embodied foundation model that serves as an agent's brain: egocentric perception, spatiotemporal grounding, physical reasoning, and planning in one model. I contributed to pre-training, Chain-of-Point reasoning, VLA, and training infrastructure.

Paper ↗Code ↗Contributions ↗RynnBrain 1.1 ↗

WorldVLA

Co-author · 2025

An action model with a built-in world model: one autoregressive network generates robot actions and predicts the frames they lead to, so the agent anticipates consequences as it acts.

Precedes RynnVLA-002; the original repository now redirects there.

Paper ↗Code ↗Model ↗

RynnVLA series

Co-author · ICRA 2026 (001)

001 pre-trains on egocentric human manipulation videos, then transfers to robot control through trajectory-aware modeling and ActionVAE. Paper · Code · Overview

002, the successor to WorldVLA, adds a continuous Action Transformer, wrist-camera input, and robot state to jointly model actions and future observations. Paper · Code · Model

JGCF
